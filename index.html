Survey & Spatial Analysis

UAV Drone Survey & Regional Coverage

This WebGIS interface visualizes primary unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) acquisition sites and administrative boundaries across the Indonesian archipelago. The active geospatial layers delineate key aerial photogrammetry corridors, topographic flight waypoints, and sovereign territorial boundaries.

Captured survey nodes cover critical geographical regions including Tabanan (Bali), Banyuwangi (East Java), Garut (West Java), Flores & Sumba (East Nusa Tenggara), and Mimika (Central Papua). These datasets support digital elevation models (DEM), dense point clouds, orthomosaic stitching, and environmental baseline monitoring.
